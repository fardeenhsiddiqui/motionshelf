# MotionShelf feedback

A short, shareable form for developers and freelancers. Send them the **Ask** section and a link to
https://motionshelf.pages.dev. It takes about 10 minutes.

This file holds the template and a log for **real** responses only. Nothing here is invented. Until someone
answers, the log is empty.

## Ask (copy this part)

> Thanks for looking at MotionShelf, a library of animated website templates for cars, off-road, motorsport and
> motorcycles. You can preview each one live, copy the code and see its license. Please spend about 10 minutes:
>
> 1. Open the home page, scroll to the bottom, and scroll through the 3D showroom.
> 2. Open **Browse**, search or filter, and open any template.
> 3. On the template page, click **Tap or click to interact**, scroll inside the preview, then press **Done**.
> 4. Try **Copy HTML** and **Download all (.zip)**.
> 5. If you can, repeat steps 1 to 3 on your phone.
>
> Then answer the questions below. Short, honest answers help most, including "I didn't notice" and "I wouldn't
> use this". Tell me what device and browser you used.

### 2. Visual quality

### 3. Animation and smoothness

- Did scrolling feel smooth, or did it ever stutter, freeze or jump? Where exactly?
- Did the 3D showroom load quickly enough? Could you rotate the car?
- Did any animation distract from what you were trying to do?
- Did the page ever feel stuck or unresponsive? What were you doing?

### 4. Mobile experience (skip if you did not try it)

- Could you scroll past the preview with your thumb without getting stuck?
- Was everything tappable (no missed taps, nothing too small)?
- Did anything overflow the screen, get cut off or look different from desktop in a way that felt wrong?
- Did you tap **Load 3D showroom**? Did it work, and was the wait acceptable?

### 5. Template usability

- Is it clear what each template includes (files, license, credits, dependencies)?
- Would you trust the license information enough to use a template in a client project? What is missing?
- Copy and download: did they work first time? Is the code readable and easy to adapt?
- Did "Copy as prompt" make sense? Would you use it?
- Which template did you like most and least, and why?

## How to use the answers

- Reproduce every bug report before fixing it, and record it in `AUDIT.md` with its root cause.
- Treat "I was confused by X" as a design problem even if nothing is broken.
- Check any change against `DESIGN_AND_ENGINEERING_CHECKLIST.md` before implementing it.
- Do not quote responses publicly without the person's permission.
