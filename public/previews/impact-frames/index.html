Use it

Copy one effect or all four

No dependencies and no images: one HTML file, one stylesheet and a few lines of script for the Hit button. Change the three colours in :root and every effect follows.

:root {
  --ink: #0b0b0f;
  --paper: #f6f3ea;
  --hit: #e5283c;
}
  • Respects prefers-reduced-motion: loops, shake and flash stop. The Hit button still shows the burst.
  • Loops pause while their panel is off screen.
  • All artwork is original and drawn in CSS. No characters, series or brands.
