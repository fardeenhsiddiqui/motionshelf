What is in the scene

Eight layers, all vector, all yours

The skyline, signs and street are inline SVG. The train, moon and rain are plain CSS. Colours come from a few custom properties at the top of the stylesheet.

  1. Sky and moonA five-stop gradient with a hazy moon that dims as the night deepens.
  2. Far skylineDark towers with a few hundred lit windows and blinking beacons.
  3. Mid towersCloser buildings with bigger windows, sliding faster.
  4. Elevated trainA lit train that crosses the scene while you scroll.
  5. Neon signsFour outlined signs that wake up in turn and flicker.
  6. Wet streetReflected colour strips, puddle rings and dashed lane marks.
  7. TravellerA figure under an umbrella with a soft reflection.
  8. RainTwo seamless tiles that loop on compositor-only transforms.

Use it

Copy it, recolour it, ship it

No dependencies and no build step: one HTML file, one stylesheet and a small script. Change the neon colours in :root and retint the signs in the markup.

:root {
  --pink: #ff2d95;
  --cyan: #2de2e6;
  --amber: #ffb000;
  --night: #070b1e;
}
  • Respects prefers-reduced-motion: the scroll scene and loops stop, a still frame remains.
  • Loops pause while the scene is off screen.
  • All artwork is original and drawn in code. No characters, series or brands.
