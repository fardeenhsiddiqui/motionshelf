What is in the scene

Eight layers, all vector, all yours

Every layer is a separate inline SVG or CSS element, so you can recolour it, swap it or move it. Colours come from a few custom properties at the top of the stylesheet.

  1. SkyA three-stop gradient, with a violet night layer that fades in.
  2. Sun and starsA radial glow that sets, and a small star field that appears late.
  3. CloudsSoft banded shapes that drift slowly on their own.
  4. Far and near hillsTwo gradient ridges that slide at different speeds.
  5. Shrine gateA simple gate with two lanterns that glow at dusk.
  6. Blossom treeA dark trunk with about 190 blossoms in three pinks.
  7. TravellerA back view figure with a scarf that sways in the wind.
  8. PetalsThirty petals falling on compositor-only transforms.

Use it

Copy it, recolour it, ship it

No dependencies and no build step: one HTML file, one stylesheet and a small script. Change the palette in :root and the whole scene follows.

:root {
  --sky-top: #241a4d;
  --sky-mid: #8a4f8f;
  --sky-low: #ff8f78;
  --blossom: #ffb3c7;
}
  • Respects prefers-reduced-motion: the scroll scene and loops stop, a still frame remains.
  • Loops pause while the scene is off screen.
  • All artwork is original and drawn in code. No characters, series or brands.
